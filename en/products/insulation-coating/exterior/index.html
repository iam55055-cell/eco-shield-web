Aerogel Insulation Coating for Exterior Walls & Roofs

에어로젤 보온·단열 도료(외벽용)

An exterior insulation coating that reflects and blocks solar heat on roofs and walls, reducing indoor heat gain, while also waterproofing and protecting against corrosion.

0.036 W/(m·K)Class A20–50 L

Features

Features

Fire, water and corrosion resistant; reflects solar radiation

High adhesion and excellent flexibility

Fast drying and curing

Free of heavy metals and formaldehyde

Benefits

Why choose it

  1. Blocks solar heat on roofs and walls to cut summer cooling loads
  2. Insulation, waterproofing and corrosion protection in one (metal roofs, sandwich panels)
  3. Ideal for re-roofing logistics centers, factories and warehouses
