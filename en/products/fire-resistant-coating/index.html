Aerogel Fire-Resistant Coatings

A thin film that buys structures critical time in a fire.

01 · Fire protection

Aerogel Fire-Resistant Coatings

Aerogel-based fire-resistant coatings that protect steel, timber, cables and concrete from flame and heat. Four products covering 30–180 minutes of fire resistance (manufacturer test basis).

No.01
Steel · Fire resistance

Aerogel Fire-Resistant Coating for Steel Structures

A fire-resistant coating that wraps structural steel in a protective film, providing up to 180 minutes of fire resistance (manufacturer test basis).

90–180 minClass A

No.02
Timber · Fire retardant

Aerogel Fire-Retardant Coating for Timber

An eco-friendly fire-retardant coating for timber structures and wood finishes in schools, hospitals and kindergartens, combining fire protection with a decorative finish.

30–120 minClass AZero formaldehyde
View details
No.03
Cables · Fire retardant

Aerogel Fire-Retardant Coating for Cables

A fire-retardant coating that stops fire from spreading along cable runs. Its flexibility prevents cracking at bends, and it can be applied directly to existing wiring.

60–120 minClass AFlexible
View details
No.04
Concrete · Fire resistance

Aerogel Fire-Resistant Coating for Concrete

A fire-resistant coating that prevents spalling and strength loss of concrete in a fire. Lighter than fire boards and easy to apply to curved surfaces such as tunnels.

60–180 minClass A

Guide

Fire-resistant vs. flame-retardant coatings

Fire-resistant coatingFlame-retardant coating
PurposeKeeps structural members (steel, concrete) load-bearing for a set time in a fireKeeps timber, cables and finishes from igniting and spreading flame
MetricFire resistance time (min)Ignition and flame-spread resistance, fire class
ECOSHIELD productsSteel (90–180 min), Concrete (60–180 min)Timber (30–120 min), Cables (60–120 min)
In KoreaKorea has separate approval schemes for fire-resistant construction and flame retardancy. Times shown are manufacturer test values; see Certifications for status.
