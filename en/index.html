Aerogel Technology

A material that stops heat
with air

Aerogel is an ultra-light solid that traps air in nanometer-scale pores. Because it is mostly air, heat has almost no path to travel, so even thin layers insulate strongly.

  • Thin and light — less thickness and weight than conventional insulation or fire boards
  • Non-combustible — Class A, inorganic silica-based materials
  • Paint, wrap, stick — coatings, blankets and film for any shape or space
