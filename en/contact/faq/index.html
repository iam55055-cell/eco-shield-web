Products

How do aerogel coatings differ from standard insulation or fire-protection coatings?
Aerogel traps air in nanoscale pores, cutting heat transfer even in thin films. ECOSHIELD coatings contain aerogel to boost insulation and fire performance.
What standards are the values based on?
All values are from the manufacturer's tests (Chinese GB standards, etc.). We do not claim compliance with EN, ASTM/UL or other regional standards unless products have been tested to them.
Who manufactures the products?
Shuoxing New Materials in China. ECOSHIELD (ECOART Co., Ltd., Korea) is the official Korea distributor.

Orders & export

What is the MOQ?
MOQ depends on the product and packaging. Tell us your target products and volumes and we will confirm.
What are the lead times?
Lead time depends on product, quantity and stock. We confirm it with every quotation.
Which Incoterms and payment terms do you offer?
FOB, CIF and DAP are available on request. Payment terms are agreed per order.
What documents are provided?
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (COO), SDS and TDS. HS codes are provided per product with your quotation.

Application

Do you provide technical support?
Yes. Send drawings and site conditions and our engineers will review specifications and recommend products and quantities.
