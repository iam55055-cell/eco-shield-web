No.11 · Aerogel Insulation Blankets

Oxidized Fiber Aerogel Blanket (YG-YYS)

산화 안정화 섬유 에어로젤 펠트(YG-YYS)

A thin, flexible aerogel blanket that reduces heat transfer between cells and modules in EV and ESS batteries, and can be cut to fit various battery designs.

1–5 mm-100 to 400 °CUL94 V-0

ManufacturerShuoxing New Materials (China)Supplied byECOSHIELD
Features

Features

Ultra-low thermal conductivity; thin, flexible and light

Thermal-shock and tear resistant

Water repellency ≥98%, excellent flame retardancy

Can be cut to shape

Benefits

Why choose it

  1. High-efficiency insulation in tight spaces to reduce heat transfer
  2. Fits many battery designs; can be cut and converted
  3. Combines flexibility, heat resistance and easy installation
Applications

Where to use it

Transportation & MobilityEV BatteriesPower & DataESS & Battery PacksPower & DataElectronics Thermal Management
