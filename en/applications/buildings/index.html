Buildings

From steel-framed buildings to sandwich-panel factories, parking garages and school gyms: improving fire safety and energy efficiency together.

Challenge

The challenge

Building fires spread quickly as steel weakens and timber or panels burn, while summer solar heat on roofs and walls drives up cooling costs.

Steel Columns & Beams

Steel does not burn, but it loses strength rapidly above roughly 500 °C. Coating columns and beams with a fire-resistant film extends the time before collapse.
