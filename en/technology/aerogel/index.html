Aerogel Technology

Why does something so thin stop heat and fire?

Aerogel TechnologyCertifications & Test Reports
What is Aerogel

A solid made
mostly of air

Aerogel is an ultra-light porous material made by replacing the liquid in a gel with gas. Though solid, it is mostly air, making it one of the best solid thermal insulators known.

Principle

How it works

Nanoporous structure

Silica particles form a 3D network with air trapped in pores tens of nanometers across.

Blocking heat transfer

Pores smaller than the mean free path of air molecules suppress convection and conduction.

Inorganic, non-combustible

Silica-based and inorganic, so it does not burn and holds its shape at high temperatures.
